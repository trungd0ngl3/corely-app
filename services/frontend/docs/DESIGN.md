## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance computational hardware: uncompromising, hyper-engineered, and tuned for elite enthusiasts. It sits at the intersection of aerospace telemetry, industrial prototyping, and cutting-edge cyber-gaming aesthetics. The emotional response is centered on adrenaline, mechanical mastery, and absolute trust in hardware stability.

The design language fuses **High-Contrast Dark Brutalism** with **Frosted Cyber-Glassmorphism**:
- Deep slate, obsidian, and carbon surfaces serve as light-absorbing backplates.
- Razor-sharp structural grid alignments, technical telemetry labels, and chamfered visual cues.
- High-chroma energy bursts powered by electric cyan and hyper-saturated thermal orange/crimson.
- Precision over decoration: tactile indicators, high-density spec readouts, and reactive micro-interactions that mirror custom liquid-cooling loops and overlocked GPU architectures.

## Colors

The palette simulates high-performance component architecture, alternating between deep thermal sinks and electrified phosphor emission.

### Functional Roles & Accents
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0E14` / `#0F131C`):** Midnight graphite and obsidian chassis tones. Grounding surfaces absorb optical noise to elevate hardware photography and metrics.
- **Electric Cyan Primary (`#00F0FF`):** Core power signature. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, active component indicators, real-time clock rates, and primary focus boundaries.
- **Neon Crimson Secondary (`#FF3E3E`):** Overclock thermal alert, high-performance mode toggle, critical clearance warnings, and cart action triggers.
- **Solar Amber Tertiary (`#FF8A00`):** Inventory depletion warnings, benchmark scores, thermal status flags, and component tiering.
- **Surface Elevation Layers:**
  - Base Board: `#0B0E14`
  - Substrate Container: `#141923`
  - Elevated Bay: `#1B2230`
  - Interactive Component Edge: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)`
  - Tactical Border Line: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy divides responsibilities across three specialized type engines:

1. **Space Grotesk (Headlines):** High-impact mechanical forms with subtle eccentricities. Drives product naming, architecture announcements, and major landing titles. Uppercase treatments on shorter headers evoke engineering blueprint schematics.
2. **Inter (Body Copy):** Maximum screen legibility for dense descriptions, warranty clauses, and compatibility guides. Ensures zero cognitive fatigue during exhaustive spec analyses.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Spec & Badges):** Engineering-grade data representation. Applied to clock frequencies, wattage readouts, bus widths, PCI-e lanes, pricing matrices, and telemetry tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 12-column fluid grid system bounded by maximum canvas constraints (1440px max desktop width), ensuring product comparison matrices and modular PC configurator bays align without horizontal drift.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px edge padding. Sticky telemetry summary bar docked at bottom.
  - `Tablet` (768px - 1024px): 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px edge padding.
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): 12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px edge padding.
- **Rhythm Model:** Strict 4px base increment. Data tables and tech spec grids condense to `space-xs` and `space-sm` for dense information density; marketing showcase sections expand to `space-xl` and above to provide atmospheric breathing room for 3D component renders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional muddy drop shadows in favor of **Luminescent Laser Planes, Glassmorphism, and Edge Refraction**:

1. **Base Plate (Level 0):** Pure solid `#0B0E14`. No elevation.
2. **Substrate Panel (Level 1):** `#141923` backed with a 1px perimeter stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating Glass Modifiers (Level 2):** Frosted glass surfaces (`rgba(20, 25, 35, 0.72)`) backed with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional top-edge highlight (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 100%)`).
4. **Active/Overclocked Elevation (Level 3):** Components actively focused or configured gain a cyan optical diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.18), inset 0 0 0 1px #00F0FF`.
5. **Modal/Configurator HUD (Level 4):** Deep background occlusion (`rgba(5, 7, 10, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), framed with high-contrast structural borders and dual-corner mechanical tick marks.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is unapologetically precision-engineered, utilizing **Soft (`1`) geometry** to echo machine-milled aluminum heat sinks, GPU shrouds, and glass-paneled chassis.

- **Standard Radius:** 4px (`0.25rem`) across input fields, spec containers, badges, and interface controls.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg`):** 8px (`0.5rem`) for main product cards, motherboard trays, and configurator panels.
- **Chamfered Cuts:** Crucial display cards, CTAs, and featured badge corners feature 45-degree mechanical angled cuts (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to reinforce industrial authenticity. Circular elements are strictly prohibited outside of hardware status LEDs and cooling fan tachometer rings.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Deploy", "Add to Rig"):** Solid Electric Cyan background (`#00F0FF`), obsidian text (`#0B0E14`), Space Grotesk 600 weight, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a laser cyan aura (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5)`). Active state applies a scale transform of `0.98`.
- **Secondary CTA ("Spec Sheet", "Compare"):** Transparent fill, 1px perimeter border in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`, text in `#00F0FF`. On hover, fills with `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)` and sharpens border opacity to 100%.
- **Destructive/Overclock Button:** Crimson fill (`#FF3E3E`) with high-contrast white text and red thermal glow (`0 0 18px rgba(255, 62, 62, 0.45)`).

### Hardware Cards & PC Builder Modules
- **Chassis Structure:** Obsidian substrate (`#141923`) with top-border energy gradient. 
- **Media Bay:** Neutral charcoal containment area with subtle carbon micro-grid backdrop. Product photography floats with hardware under-glow.
- **Status/Inventory Badges:** JetBrains Mono uppercase micro-pills positioned top-right (e.g., `[IN STOCK: 3 UNITS]`, `[PCIE 5.0 READY]`).
- **Telemetry Readout Strip:** Lower section of the card displays key specifications in a structured matrix (Boost Clock, VRAM, TDP) utilizing JetBrains Mono with subdued labels and glowing primary values.

### Glassmorphic Filters & Hardware Selectors
- Frosted HUD floating sidebar utilizing `rgba(17, 22, 32, 0.65)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`.
- Sliders for TDP (Wattage) and Price feature electric cyan active rail fills with glowing square thumb nodes.

### Inputs, Checkboxes & Telemetry Switches
- **Input Fields:** Dark slate backdrop (`#0F131C`), recessed 1px inner stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Focus shifts border to `#00F0FF` with a subtle inner glow. Text inputs display monospaced values for numeric parameters.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp 2px rounded square boxes with cyber-cyan check glyphs and mechanical click feedback.
- **Hardware Toggle Switches:** Industrial rocker design showing binary `[0 / 1]` state indicators powered by JetBrains Mono typography.

### Specialized Hardware Components
- **Bottleneck / Compatibility Matrix Indicator:** Visual status rail showing power budget overhead, CPU/GPU balance ratio, and physical clearance confirmations (e.g., `PASS: 360mm Radiator Clear`).
- **Live Wattage & Thermal Meter:** Real-time system load bar that scales dynamically from Cyan (nominal) to Solar Amber (80% load) to Neon Crimson (thermal throttle limit).
